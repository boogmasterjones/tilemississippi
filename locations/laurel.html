Tile Work for Laurel's Historic Homes and Growing Neighborhoods

Laurel's housing stock stands out in the Pine Belt for its concentration of early-1900s craftsman and historic homes near downtown, alongside newer construction further out. Renovating a historic Laurel home for tile work often means matching period-appropriate patterns and working around older plumbing and uneven original subfloors — a different job than tiling a newer build.

Whether you're restoring a historic bathroom in a Laurel craftsman home or updating a kitchen backsplash in a newer subdivision, we bring the same care to both: bathroom, shower, kitchen, floor, and outdoor tile installation done with attention to the home's specific age and construction.

Common Questions

Laurel Tile Installation FAQ

Can you match tile patterns in an older Laurel home?

We can often source similar period-style tile for historic Laurel homes, though an exact match to original tile isn't always possible. We'll walk you through the best options for your specific house.

How far is Laurel from Hattiesburg?

Laurel is about 30 miles northeast of Hattiesburg, one of our farther-reaching Pine Belt service areas alongside Columbia and Wiggins.

Do you handle full bathroom remodels in historic homes, not just tile?

We focus specifically on tile installation. For a full remodel involving plumbing, electrical, or structural work, we can often coordinate with other trades or point you to someone local we trust.
