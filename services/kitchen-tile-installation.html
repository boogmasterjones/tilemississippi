Backsplashes and Kitchen Floors That Hold Up

A kitchen backsplash is one of the most visible design elements in the whole house — and one of the trickiest to install cleanly, thanks to outlets, cabinet edges, and often an accent pattern or mosaic. We plan the layout before setting tile, so grout lines land symmetrically around the range hood and cabinets instead of trailing off at an awkward cut on one end.

Kitchen floors have a different challenge: constant foot traffic, dropped pans, and spills. Porcelain and ceramic tile installed with a proper substrate hold up to that daily wear far better than most other flooring, provided the subfloor prep and grout work are done correctly from the start.

What We Install

  • Backsplash tile installation, including mosaic and accent patterns
  • Kitchen floor tile in porcelain, ceramic, and stone-look finishes
  • Precise outlet, switch, and cabinet-edge cutouts
  • Removal of old backsplash or flooring
  • Grout sealing for easy cleanup around cooking areas
  • Transition strips between kitchen tile and adjoining rooms

Choosing a Layout That Works With Your Cabinets

Backsplash layout should be planned around your range hood and any focal point, not just started from one corner of the wall. We dry-lay a run of tile first to check spacing, especially with larger-format tile or a herringbone/mosaic pattern, before committing with adhesive.

Common Questions

Kitchen Tile FAQ

How long does a backsplash installation take?

A standard kitchen backsplash typically takes 1–2 days, including layout, setting, and grouting. More intricate mosaic or pattern work can take longer.

Can you install tile flooring without removing my cabinets?

In most kitchens, yes — flooring is installed up to the cabinet edges (tiling "around" rather than under cabinets is standard practice). We'll walk through the specifics for your layout during the quote.

What tile size works best for a small kitchen backsplash?

It depends on the look you want — smaller mosaic tile adds texture and detail, while larger-format tile creates a cleaner, more seamless look with fewer grout lines. We'll bring samples so you can compare in your own kitchen light.
